# 🏫 School / College Website

A modern, responsive, and bilingual website designed for schools and colleges to share academic information, announcements, events, and important resources with students, parents, and visitors.

Built using HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript, with Google Sheets and Google Apps Script for database management.

## ✨ Key Features

- Responsive school website design.
- English and Nepali language support.
- Principal profile and school information.
- Academic programs and class subjects.
- Online admission and contact forms.
- Notices and announcements.
- Photo gallery and image albums.
- Downloadable academic PDFs and admission documents.
- Bikram Sambat (BS) calendar with AD date support.
- Automatic Nepal-time clock.
- Light and Dark mode.
- Keyboard-friendly navigation.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- Vanilla JavaScript
- Google Apps Script
- Google Sheets
- Google Drive
- Nepali Date Converter

## 🔐 Database & Administration

Google Sheets is used to manage school information and form submissions, while Google Drive stores images and PDF documents.

Google Apps Script connects the website with the database and manages:

- School notices and announcements.
- Academic programs and subjects.
- Gallery images.
- Admission documents.
- Contact and admission enquiries.
- Automatic email notifications for new submissions.

Authorized administrators manage website content through Google Sheets and Drive.

## 🎯 Project Purpose

To provide schools and colleges with a simple digital platform for sharing information, managing academic resources, communicating with parents and students, and making school services more accessible.
